Before you begin

A few useful answers.

A project request starts a conversation. It does not commit you to a purchase.

Is Renue Home a remodeling contractor?

No. Renue Home is an advertising marketplace and matching service. We share project requests with independent providers. Any estimate, installation or agreement is handled directly by the provider you choose.

Will I see an instant price?

No. The provider needs to understand your space, materials and scope to prepare pricing. Your request can start that conversation; an in-home consultation may be needed for an accurate quote.

What types of projects can I request?

You can select a tub-to-shower conversion, a walk-in or replacement shower, a full bathroom remodel, or a safety and mobility update. Available services and designs depend on your location and the provider.

What happens after I send my request?

Your information may be shared with our marketing partners, and you may be contacted about your project according to the consent terms shown before submission. Matching is subject to provider availability; a match is not guaranteed.

Is there a fee or obligation?

Renue Home does not charge homeowners to submit a quote request. There is no obligation to hire a provider. Review all project prices, terms, credentials and warranties directly with the provider before making a decision.
